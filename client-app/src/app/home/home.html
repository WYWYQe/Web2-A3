Together we can make a difference

Charity Events connects generous people with local fundraising events — fun runs, gala dinners, silent auctions, concerts and community quiz nights. Browse what is on, find a cause you care about, and join in.

Our mission

We believe every community is stronger when people show up for one another. Our mission is to make local charitable causes visible, accessible and easy to support.

How it works

Search for an event near you, read the full details, and register your interest. Every ticket purchased acts as a donation to the cause behind the event.
